Important: The Hair Thinning Guide provides general educational and informational content. It is not medical advice and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition.

1. General information only

The information provided on this website and in the Hair Thinning Guide is intended to help readers understand general information about hair thinning, possible contributing factors and commonly discussed approaches.

Individual causes of hair thinning can vary considerably. Information in the guide should not be treated as a diagnosis or as a personalised treatment plan.

2. Not a substitute for medical advice

Nothing on this website or in the guide should be considered a substitute for advice from a qualified doctor, pharmacist, dermatologist or other appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

You should seek professional medical advice if you are concerned about hair loss, changes in your hair or scalp, or your general health.

3. Do not rely on the guide for diagnosis

The Hair Thinning Guide cannot determine the cause of an individual's hair loss.

Hair loss can have different causes, and some forms of hair loss can occasionally be associated with an underlying medical issue. The NHS advises people who are worried about hair loss to speak to a GP.

4. Treatment and products

The guide may discuss treatments, products, ingredients or approaches that are commonly discussed in relation to hair thinning.

Mentioning a treatment, product or approach does not mean that it is suitable for everyone, that it will work for a particular person, or that it is recommended by the Hair Thinning Guide.

You should discuss treatments and medicines with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional before deciding whether they are suitable for you.

5. Individual results may vary

No particular result from using any product, treatment, routine or approach discussed on this website or in the guide is guaranteed.

Hair growth and hair loss can be affected by many individual factors, and experiences can differ from person to person.

6. Emergency or urgent concerns

Do not rely on this website for urgent medical concerns. If you have a serious or rapidly developing health problem, seek appropriate medical attention rather than relying on information provided by this website.

7. Accuracy of information

We aim to provide information that is clear, responsible and useful. However, medical and scientific knowledge can change over time and we cannot guarantee that every piece of information will always be complete, current or appropriate for every individual.

You should check important medical information with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

Purchasing or reading the Hair Thinning Guide does not guarantee any particular outcome, improvement or change in hair growth or hair loss.

9. Professional advice

If you are considering treatment for hair thinning or hair loss, speak to an appropriately qualified healthcare professional who can consider your individual circumstances.
