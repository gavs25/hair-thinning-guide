What Causes Hair Thinning?

There is no single cause of thinning hair. Different types of hair loss can have different explanations, which is why identifying the pattern and circumstances can be useful.

Hereditary Hair Thinning

Some forms of hair loss run in families and can produce gradual changes in hair density or a widening parting, receding hairline or thinning at the crown.

Age-Related Hair Changes

Hair characteristics can change as people get older. Hair may become finer or growth patterns may change.

Hormonal & Other Factors

Hormonal changes, illness, stress, nutritional problems, some medicines and other factors can sometimes be associated with hair loss.

Changes in Hair or Scalp

Changes affecting the scalp or hair itself may also be relevant. Unusual, sudden or unexplained changes should be discussed with an appropriate healthcare professional.

Thinning Hair: What Should You Consider?

If you have noticed more hair in your brush, shower or on your pillow, or your hair appears less dense than it used to be, you may be wondering what causes hair thinning and what you can do about it.

How do you know if your hair is thinning?

Changes can sometimes be noticed gradually. Examples may include increased visibility of the scalp, a wider parting, a changing hairline, reduced density at the crown or an overall reduction in hair volume. These signs do not identify a specific cause by themselves.

What are the options for thinning hair?

The appropriate approach depends on the underlying cause. Treatment and management options can differ considerably between different types of hair loss. A healthcare professional can help determine whether further assessment is appropriate.

What about hair care?

Gentle hair care can be sensible when hair feels fragile or breaks easily. Avoiding unnecessary damage from excessive heat, harsh chemical treatments or tight hairstyles may be worth considering.

When should you get professional advice?

Consider seeking professional advice if hair loss is sudden, significant, unexplained, patchy or accompanied by other concerning symptoms. An appropriate professional can assess the situation rather than relying on an online guide alone.

Hair Thinning Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about thinning hair, hair loss and the Hair Thinning Guide.

What causes hair thinning?

Hair thinning can have many possible causes, including hereditary patterns, ageing, hormonal changes, illness, stress, nutritional issues and some medicines. The cause of an individual's hair loss cannot be diagnosed by this website.

What can I do about thinning hair?

Understanding the possible cause is an important first step. Depending on the situation, people may discuss treatment and management options with a qualified healthcare professional.

Is hair thinning normal as you get older?

Hair characteristics can change with age, but significant, sudden or unexpected hair loss can have many possible causes and may warrant professional assessment.

Does this guide diagnose hair loss?

No. The guide provides general educational information and cannot diagnose an individual's hair loss.
